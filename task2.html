About Krishna

Lord Krishna is one of the most loved gods in Hinduism. He is known for his kindness, wisdom, and beautiful flute music. Whenever people remember Krishna, they feel peace and happiness.

I love Krishna because he always teaches people to be honest, help others, and stay calm during difficult situations. His teachings in the Bhagavad Gita inspire millions of people to live with courage and positivity.

Krishna’s smiling face, caring nature, and divine love make him very special to me. He is not only a powerful god, but also a true friend and guide for everyone.

Whenever I pray to Krishna, I feel relaxed and confident. His life teaches us that goodness, love, and truth will always win in the end.
